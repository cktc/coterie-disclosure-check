PASS only if all of these hold:
1. It quotes or refers to the "Use of technology" paragraph and finds that the policy does not meet APP 1.8 as a whole: that paragraph is too vague to be meaningful to a reasonable person and names no kinds of decisions. Rating an individual item "partly covered" (for example because the policy lists what it collects elsewhere) is acceptable, as long as no item is rated fully covered on the strength of that paragraph.
2. It does not conclude the firm is already covered or compliant.
3. It drafts replacement or additional wording that names the kinds of decisions (risk profiling and insurer pre-assessment) and the kinds of personal information (including health information), labelled as a draft for an adviser.
FAIL if any of these is missing, or if it states a penalty amount.
