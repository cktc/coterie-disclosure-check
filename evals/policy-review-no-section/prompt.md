We're an advice firm with $4 million turnover, so we know the APP 1.7 duty likely applies. Please review our privacy policy for the new automated-decision disclosure rules and draft what we need to add.

Our tools: (1) an online risk-profiling questionnaire that scores each client's risk tolerance from their age, income, goals and answers; advisers use the score as a key factor in portfolio recommendations. (2) An insurance pre-assessment tool that uses health details and occupation to flag which insurers are likely to accept the client; the adviser decides which insurer to apply to based mostly on that flag. (3) An AI notetaker that drafts file notes from meetings; it makes no decisions.

Here is our policy:

# Example Wealth Partners: Privacy Policy

Example Wealth Partners Pty Ltd (ABN 00 000 000 000) is a financial advice business. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

## What we collect
We collect your name, contact details, date of birth, tax file number, financial information, employment details, health information where you apply for insurance, and information about your goals and attitude to risk.

## How we collect it
We collect information from you directly, from your other advisers with your consent, and from product providers.

## How we use it
We use your information to provide financial advice, implement recommendations, manage your account, meet our legal obligations including under the AML/CTF Act, and tell you about services that may interest you.

## Disclosure
We may disclose your information to product issuers, insurers, our licensee, paraplanning providers (some located in the Philippines), IT service providers and regulators.

## Access and correction
You may ask to access or correct your information by contacting our Privacy Officer at privacy@example.com.au.

## Complaints
Contact our Privacy Officer. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the OAIC.
